Estimator

Every episode is drawn from one proposal q = 0.4·P1 + 0.15·(P2 + P4 + P5 + P6) and simulated once, deterministically in (x, seed). For each candidate prior p the campaign reports the self-normalised importance estimate P̂(fail) = Σ wᵢ 1[failᵢ] / Σ wᵢ, wᵢ = p(xᵢ)/q(xᵢ), with a bootstrap 95 % CI over episodes and the Kish effective sample size ESS = (Σw)² / Σw². An estimate is marked reliable only if ESS ≥ and at least half of p's own mass lies inside the simulated box (support coverage). CEM samples are kept in the campaign for replay but never enter these weighted estimates.
